# SOP-001: Escalation Matrix (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
Level 1 (operator/tech): stops under 10 minutes, known fixes in the SOPs.
Level 2 (line engineer): repeated stops, defect rate above 2 percent for one hour, any SOP step that fails.
Level 3 (process or vision engineer): trend across shifts, defect rate above 3 percent, suspected equipment or lot issue.
Level 4 (plant manager): safety issue, customer shipment at risk, quarantine of more than one shift of product.
Write an escalation report with timeline, evidence, containment, root cause, and next steps.
