# SOP-118: Feeder Jam Recovery (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
Applies to: vibratory bowl and linear feeders.
Symptoms: short stops, parts stuck in the track, feeder_jam downtime events.
Steps:
1. Lock out the feeder, clear the track, and inspect parts for burrs or bent terminals.
2. Check bowl amplitude and track tooling for wear.
3. Check incoming material for out-of-spec parts.
4. Restart and watch 10 cycles.
Log repeated jams on the same feeder for the weekly maintenance review.
